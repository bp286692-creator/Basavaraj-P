Our Heritage & Craft

The Art of Patient Fermentation

We started with a century-old wild sourdough culture and an uncompromising belief: real bread requires only four honest ingredients, time, and human hands.

Founded on 12th Main Indiranagar by baker Vikram and pastry chef Ananya, Artisan Crumb was conceived as an homage to the slow bakeries of rural France, re-imagined through the lens of Southern India's agricultural riches.

Every single loaf begins its journey 36 hours prior with our heritage levain starter, nicknamed "Bessie". We source organic heirloom wheat and ancient Karnataka red millets, milling them gently so the vital wheat germ oils and complex aromas stay intact.

You won't find dough conditioners, commercial yeast accelerants, or artificial preservatives in our kitchen. Just French deck stone heat, wild native yeasts, and the golden crackle of a blistered, caramelized crust.

Chef Vikram & Ananya
Head Baker & Master Pâtissière

36-Hour Wild Starter

Extended cold-proofing breaks down phytates and complex starches, creating an open crumb that is exceptionally gut-friendly.

Normandy Cultured Butter

Imported 84% butterfat European cultured butter hand-laminated to create 81 gossamer-thin, shattered flake layers.

Local Karnataka Terroir

Coorg single-estate vanilla beans, Chikmagalur dark roasts, and Wayanad forest honey infused into our curds and glazes.

Zero Preservatives

No potassium bromate, artificial dough softeners, or bleach. Pure stone-ground flour, Himalayan pink salt, and spring water.
